📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance across different products and regions to identify profitability risks and provide actionable business recommendations.
The dashboard is designed to help decision-makers quickly understand where the business is performing well and where corrective actions are required.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze sales, profit, and loss patterns across regions and products

Identify loss-making and break-even transactions

Highlight high-risk products and regions

Provide data-driven action recommendations such as Continue, Review, or Stop

🛠 Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Dashboard design, KPIs, visual analytics

Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and calculations

📈 Key KPIs

Total Sales (₹)

Total Profit (₹)

Profit Margin (%)

Loss Transactions Count

These KPIs provide an executive-level summary of overall business performance.

📊 Dashboard Features
🔹 Page 1: Executive Summary

KPI cards for quick business health assessment

Product Risk Analysis showing Profit, Break-even and Loss distribution

Regional Profitability Overview to compare region-wise performance

Action Recommendation Distribution to support decision-making

Interactive slicers for filtering by Region and Product

🔹 Page 2: Detailed Analysis

Product–Region Performance Details table

Displays profitability status and recommended business actions

Enables deeper investigation of specific product-region combinations

💡 Key Insights

Certain regions show consistent losses for climate-dependent products, indicating demand mismatch.

Some products perform well across all regions and should be continued or expanded.

Loss-making product-region combinations are clearly flagged for review or discontinuation.
